This week at a glance

Nordic financial services firms, led by Danske Bank, are accelerating AI-led transformation at scale, with investments spanning enterprise data integration, colleague productivity, customer experience, and wealth management platforms such as BlackRock's Aladdin Wealth. The appointment of dedicated technology and data leadership at Danske Bank signals that AI strategy is now a board-level priority, moving from experimentation toward structured execution under multi-year programmes like Forward '28. In parallel, security and digital access infrastructure is maturing rapidly, with ASSA ABLOY's ISO 27001 certification and smart access portfolio reflecting the broader expectation from financial services clients that technology partners meet rigorous cybersecurity and compliance standards. For a TAM advising this portfolio, the key strategic thread is that AI adoption and digital transformation are only as credible as the security and governance foundations beneath them, making it essential to connect conversations about innovation with conversations about risk, compliance, and trusted infrastructure.
