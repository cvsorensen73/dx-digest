This week at a glance

Nordic financial services firms are accelerating AI-led transformation at pace, with Danske Bank exemplifying the trend through its Forward '28 strategy, new technology and data leadership appointments, and the adoption of BlackRock's Aladdin Wealth platform — signaling that AI is moving from experimentation into core product and investment infrastructure. A persistent gap between customer expectations and actual AI-driven personalization remains a strategic pressure point, suggesting that execution and change management are as critical as technology selection. Beyond banking, ASSA ABLOY's capital markets activity and interoperability push in mobile access technology reflect a broader theme of digitizing physical security infrastructure, which is increasingly relevant to financial institutions managing branch networks, data centers, and hybrid workplace estates. For a TAM in this space, the priority conversations should center on helping clients close the strategy-to-execution gap in AI, ensuring technology governance keeps pace with rapid tooling decisions, and connecting physical and digital security investments into a coherent operational resilience narrative.
