This week at a glance

Nordic financial services firms are accelerating AI and cloud adoption as a core strategic priority, with Danske Bank's multi-year AWS commitment and deliberate AI talent investment in Poland signaling that transformation is now being executed at scale rather than piloted. The emphasis on using AI to enhance both colleague productivity and customer experience reflects a dual mandate that TAMs should position cloud and AI partnerships around, since internal efficiency gains and external service differentiation are being pursued in parallel. Across the broader portfolio, digital certification and compliance readiness are becoming competitive differentiators, as seen in Assa Abloy securing ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for its digital solutions, a trend that financial services clients will increasingly expect from their own technology and vendor ecosystems. For a TAM advising in this space, the strategic imperative is clear: clients are moving from strategy to execution on AI and cloud, making governance frameworks, talent models, and certified partner ecosystems the critical enablers to address in near-term engagements.
