This week at a glance

Across the portfolio, agentic AI is emerging as the defining theme of the week, with Danske Bank completing Denmark's first AI agent payment in partnership with Mastercard and deepening its AWS relationship to accelerate enterprise AI capabilities under its Forward '28 strategy. This signals that Nordic financial institutions are moving beyond AI experimentation into production-grade, autonomous systems with real transactional stakes, raising immediate questions around governance, compliance, and vendor dependency. In parallel, Assa Abloy's pivot toward certified digital solutions and its Capital Markets Day messaging reflect a broader pattern where even hardware-oriented firms are reframing their value proposition around software and digital services to offset cyclical market pressures. For a TAM advising this portfolio, the strategic priority is helping clients build the organisational scaffolding, cloud architecture, and risk frameworks needed to responsibly operationalise AI at scale, while recognising that digital transformation is no longer confined to pure-play financial services but is reshaping adjacently regulated sectors as well.
