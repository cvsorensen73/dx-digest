This week at a glance

Nordic financial services firms are accelerating AI adoption at scale, with Danske Bank's expanded AWS partnership and Aladdin Wealth implementation signaling a clear strategic shift toward cloud-native, AI-driven banking infrastructure and personalized investment services.
