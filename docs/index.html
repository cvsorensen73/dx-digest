This week at a glance

Across the portfolio, AI adoption is emerging as the dominant strategic theme, with Danske Bank making especially aggressive moves through new generative AI leadership, an expanded AWS partnership, and a sustained investment push tied to its Forward '28 transformation programme. This signals that Nordic financial services firms are moving beyond AI experimentation into structural, multi-year commitments that touch both workforce capability and core technology infrastructure. For Assa Abloy, the parallel trends of smart security integration and sustainability as a growth driver reflect a broader market expectation that digital and physical security must converge, with cloud-connected platforms like the Aperio-Axis integration pointing toward the kind of ecosystem thinking that financial services clients will increasingly demand from their security and access vendors. As a TAM advising these firms, the key strategic implication is that customers are accelerating platform consolidation and partnership depth rather than point-solution buying, making it critical to position your organisation as a long-term transformation partner with clear AI and sustainability credentials rather than a transactional supplier.
