This week at a glance

Nordic financial services firms are accelerating AI adoption at scale, with Danske Bank's expanded AWS partnership and new generative AI leadership appointments signaling a strategic shift from experimentation to enterprise-wide deployment under its Forward '28 productivity agenda. Cloud infrastructure is emerging as the critical enabler of this transformation, reinforcing the importance of hyperscaler relationships as a core pillar of digital strategy rather than a tactical IT decision. Outside of financial services, ASSA ABLOY's Capital Markets Day and continued portfolio expansion through acquisitions like Sargent & Greenleaf highlight how even hardware-adjacent firms in the portfolio are using strategic events to signal long-term digital and smart security ambitions, relevant context for a TAM advising on convergence between physical security and digital identity. For a TAM serving Nordic financial institutions, the overarching theme is that AI readiness is now a boardroom and organizational design question as much as a technology one, making leadership capability, cloud partnership governance, and productivity measurement the most strategically relevant conversations to be having with clients right now.
